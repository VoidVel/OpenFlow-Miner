      What a key is not. There's no account, password, or email anywhere in OpenFlow Miner.
      A key can't be listed, looked up, or revoked through any endpoint, and it grants no access that
      keyless callers don't already have — only a higher request rate. Keys expire on their own and, on the
      demo instance, are held in memory only.
